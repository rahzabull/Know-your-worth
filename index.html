How we price your photo

An image recognition model (MobileNet, trained to recognise about 1,000 kinds of objects and scenes) runs right here in your browser. Your photo is never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

We match what the model sees to the kinds of audiences advertisers pay to reach, then stage an auction between made-up bidders. The final price is the highest bid.

What moves the price

What we spotExampleBids
Everyday interestsFood, gadgets, fashion, fitness$0.20 to $1

Life eventsA wedding, a baby, a move$2 to $9

We also read the hidden details saved inside the file, like where and when it was taken and on which phone. Location raises the price most, because advertisers pay extra for it. This happens on your device too, and we never put your location on the share card.

Advertisers pay the most for people whose spending is about to change, which is why life events jump in price. The tiers reflect how ad pricing works in general; the exact amounts are illustrative, not real quotes.

What we never guess

Age, gender, ethnicity, health, religion, sexuality or anything about children. The model here can't, and we wouldn't want it to.

Why Ente made this

Whoever stores your photos can see what's in them. Ente encrypts your photos on your device, so we can't see them at all.
